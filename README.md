Problem Statement:

Space enthusiasts and learners often struggle to find a single, user-friendly place to keep track of astronomical events and explore the cosmos. Currently, information about upcoming celestial occurrences, planet details, and star maps is frequently scattered across various websites or presented in a dry, complicated way. There is a need for a unified, interactive platform that brings the universe directly to the user. By combining a clear celestial event calendar with interactive star maps and engaging planet profiles, this platform will solve the problem of scattered information, providing a visually immersive and easy-to -use space for anyone to learn about astronomy.
